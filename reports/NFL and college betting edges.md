# Execution, not forecasting, wins football betting

For an automated NFL and FBS bettor in Arizona, the edges with real evidence behind them are **price and execution edges**: buying soft-book prices that lag a sharp reference, cutting the cost of each bet, harvesting promotions that are +EV by construction, and keeping betting accounts alive long enough to use all of these. Forecasting edges against the closing line remain undocumented in both sports, and live betting has the weakest case of all for an hourly, API-driven system. The project already holds the best-documented edge in the field. Its CFB soft-book-vs-Pinnacle result (+2-4% CLV, largest 3-7 days out) closely matches the cleanest public analogue: Data Golf's +1.8% from betting other books against Pinnacle's opening price. The project has also already tested most of the structural ideas the literature offers: middles, arbitrage, teasers, point buys, alternate lines, first-half and team-total derivatives, yardage and touchdown props, and win totals. That leaves a short list of untested ideas, ranked here by expected value and fit. First, fix the fair price itself. Every track devigs multiplicatively, the method most biased on lopsided moneylines, and its "sharp median" mostly averages books that copy Pinnacle. Second, post maker orders on exchanges instead of crossing the spread, since making costs about a quarter of taking on Kalshi and exchanges do not limit winners. Third, run a boost and bonus-bet engine driven by the existing fair line. Fourth, paper-track season-long prop unders. Fifth, extend the forecast-wind under track to FBS. Sixth, price CFB derivatives against the CFB sharp line. Three outside facts discount everything above. Arizona's legal fight with Kalshi is unresolved after the Ninth Circuit sided with states in August 2026. US books admit they cut stakes for bettors who beat the close. And from 2026 only 90% of gambling losses are tax-deductible, which penalizes high-turnover, thin-margin strategies.

## Prices lag, forecasts don't, and the project already sits where prices lag

The best public evidence on market structure comes from outside football, but it is unusually clean. Data Golf tracked more than 106,000 golf matchups and found that Pinnacle's closing odds have a "1 to 1 relationship between expected and actual ROI." DraftKings and Bet365 showed "only a slightly positive relationship," and BetOnline, 5Dimes and Bovada "largely copy Pinnacle or Bet365." **Betting other books with Pinnacle's opening odds as the fair price returned about +1.8%**, and the return fell as the edge threshold rose ([Data Golf](https://datagolf.com/how-sharp-are-bookmakers)). That is the same mechanism as the project's CFB track and about the same size, which makes the +2-4% CLV plausible rather than suspicious. It also warns that tighter filters buy less than they seem to. Football's market-setting order explains when this edge appears. **Circa posts the first college football line every Sunday**, at low limits, and "the most respected professional bettors will dictate what the betting line is, not the bookmaker" ([Circa](https://www.circalasvegas.com/blog/sportsbook/from-the-experts/article/how-lines-are-set-an-oddsmakers-perspective/)). Soft US books follow sharp moves with a lag a data vendor puts at 15-60 seconds for DraftKings and FanDuel and 30-90 seconds for Caesars. That figure comes without published methodology, and The Odds API's own collection interval of roughly 30-60 seconds means a pipeline polling it cannot see those windows ([SharpAPI](https://sharpapi.io/learn/how-live-is-real-time-odds-data)). The project's edge therefore comes from slower mispricings that sit for hours or days early in the week. Its own hourly study found soft-vs-sharp opportunities last about 5-6 hours, which confirms that. Steam-chasing would need a sub-second feed, and experienced bettors advise that stale-line pickoffs are "not worth burning an account" ([Betting Life](https://betting-life-newsletter.beehiiv.com/p/picking-off-stale-lines)).

On the forecasting side, no source documents a public model beating NFL or CFB closing lines over a large, out-of-sample, verified record. The best-supported public practice is to **shrink toward the market**. nfelo's best flat blend is 65% market and 35% model, and an error-weighted version that shrinks harder on teams where the model has recently trailed the market beats both on Brier score ([nfelo](https://www.nfeloapp.com/analysis/using-market-regression-to-improve-prediction-accuracy-in-the-nfl/)). The CFBD community's 714-feature gradient-boosted model, which used pregame spreads as inputs, went 50% against DraftKings openers over 127 games ([CFBD](https://blog.collegefootballdata.com/predicting-spreads-gbdt/)). A 2024 review found most ML football papers report accuracy, not returns against the close, and stressed that selecting models for calibration produces better betting returns than selecting for accuracy ([arXiv 2410.21484](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.21484)). Peer-reviewed market anomalies exist, but they are old, unstable or both. NFL moneylines in 2007-16 showed a *reverse* favorite-longshot bias that was "unpredictable and vary by season" ([Journal of Economic Insight](https://journalofeconomicinsight.com/index.php/joei/article/view/1335)). CFB interconference spreads were inefficient for certain conferences over 2003-16 ([Moore & Francisco](https://ideas.repec.org/a/kap/atlecj/v47y2019i2d10.1007_s11293-019-09616-7.html)). CFB spreads predict less well in bowls than in the regular season ([Cox et al.](https://ideas.repec.org/a/sae/jospec/v22y2021i3p251-273.html)). The project's failed CFB situational and opener-disagreement rules are what this literature predicts.

How professionals operate points the same way. Billy Walters' published process does include ratings: a 90/10 weekly update, a QB worth "about a touchdown," and 60% of players worth "basically zero." But most of his rules are about execution: "bet favorites early and dogs late," hold "as many accounts as you can," cap any event at 3% of bankroll, and size in half-units from 0.5 to 3 ([Covers](https://www.covers.com/guides/betting-tips-from-pro-sports-bettor-billy-walters)). Westgate SuperContest winners describe selective weekly cards built on late qualitative information: beat writers, practice reports, film ([TheLines](https://www.thelines.com/westgate-supercontest-winner-tips/)). The Computer Group's 1980s college football edge came from computer models in an era of hand-made lines. It reportedly won nearly $5M in documented 1983-84 wagers ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Kent_(computer_specialist))), and that gap has since closed in the main markets.

Measured against this map, the project's coverage is broad. Its own studies found the following. True middles exist but average −5% to −6% per two-bet package, and the +EV ones are worth about +0.1 unit per season per market (`output/research/middles_segments.md`). Same-number arbitrage among allowed books was absent in 2020-25. Wong teasers lose at today's −130/−140 pricing. Half-point buys are never +EV. Opening alt ladders and opening derivatives do not clear the 4-5% hold. The best first-half/team-total rule kept only a small positive CLV out of sample: +0.86% ± 0.16 on 48 bets in 2024-25 (`output/research/derivatives.md`). Yardage and touchdown props failed, and only Friday receptions shopping passed on CLV, at +2.7%. Model projections of preseason win totals carry no information beyond the posted total. The exchange track bets as a taker only. What remains untested sits mostly in execution: how the fair price is computed, which side of the order book to stand on, promotions, and account management.

## Fees, legality, limits and taxes now set the size of any edge

Exchanges have repriced themselves around probability-dependent fees that peak exactly where football spreads and totals trade. **Kalshi's taker fee is 0.07 × P × (1−P) per contract, or 1.75¢ at 50¢, about 3.5% of stake. The maker fee uses 0.0175 instead, about 0.9% of stake, and cancelling a resting order is free** ([River Markets](https://www.rivermarkets.com/insights/kalshi-fees.html)). The 2025 change raised fees about 15% on NFL, NBA and NHL markets because those cluster near 50/50 ([InGame](https://www.ingame.com/kalshis-change-may-increase-fee-sports-traders/)). A weekly pricing study measured Kalshi's all-in taker cost on NFL moneylines and totals at **4.31%, against 4.41% at FanDuel and 4.52% at DraftKings**, so taking on Kalshi is no cheaper than a retail book ([RotoWire](https://www.rotowire.com/article/fanduel-kalshi-pull-even-on-nfl-week-3-pricing-134248)). Polymarket's sports fee peaks at 0.75% at 50% and pays makers a 25% rebate ([iGaming Business](https://igamingbusiness.com/prediction-markets/polymarket-sports-fee-hike-2026/)). Robinhood's Rothera contracts cost at most 1¢ per contract ([Covers](https://www.covers.com/industry/robinhood-launches-world-cup-contracts-through-rothera-june-5-2026)). Sources disagree on ProphetX: "up to ~1%" of net winnings ([LegalSportsReport](https://www.legalsportsreport.com/dfs-sites/prophetx-vs-novig/)) versus 2% ([PredictionScout](https://predictionscout.com/reviews/prophetx-review/)). Either way it costs about 0.5-1% of stake at even money, and it lists NCAA football. Novig charges zero on pregame trades but **excludes Arizona** ([BettingUSA](https://www.bettingusa.com/prediction-markets/reviews/novig/)). Sporttrade stopped taking Arizona wagers in May 2026 ([BetArizona](https://www.betarizona.com/sports-betting/sporttrade)).

The best microstructure study of Kalshi covers 46,282 contracts from 2021-25 and is mostly pre-sports. **Makers averaged −9.64% after fees and takers −31.46%. Contracts priced at 1-10¢ lost more than 60%, and contracts at 50¢ and above earned small positive returns**, about +2.6% for makers ([GWU working paper](https://www2.gwu.edu/~forcpgm/2026-001.pdf)). A separate study of about 23 million NBA, MLB and NHL Kalshi trades found prices well calibrated mid-game but diverging sharply in the final ten minutes before settlement, which the author attributes to "insurance" demand from traders holding losing positions ([arXiv 2607.14430](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.14430)). Both point to the same conclusion. On an exchange, the structural profit goes to whoever supplies liquidity at a fair price, not to whoever takes it. Depth is uneven, which helps a quoter. Sunday Night Football drew $56.3M in Kalshi game-winner volume ([InGame](https://www.ingame.com/kalshi-record-football-weekend/)), yet an August 2026 snapshot showed only $107.59 at the top of a Week 1 book ([OddsPapi](https://oddspapi.io/blog/?p=3660)). The ladder is thin at the best price even where flow is heavy.

Arizona makes all of this provisional. The state filed 20 criminal counts against Kalshi in March 2026. A federal judge blocked enforcement in May. In late August the Ninth Circuit held that states can regulate Kalshi's sports contracts ([Public Gaming](https://publicgaming.com/news-categories-m/legal/16348-arizona-ninth-circuit-ruling-backs-state-authority-over-kalshi-sports-contracts-but-election-dispute-remains); [Front Office Sports](https://frontofficesports.com/article/arizona-sues-kalshi-after-kalshi-sued-arizona/)). Crypto.com has already geofenced Arizona, and the Arizona Department of Gaming moved to revoke Underdog's DFS license for "aiding and abetting" it ([iGB](https://igamingbusiness.com/legal-compliance/arizona-pulls-underdog-dfs-license-prediction-market-rift/)). Any exchange strategy should assume access could end suddenly and keep balances sized for that.

Licensed books are the other constraint. In Massachusetts, operators told the regulator that bettors who "consistently beat the closing line are more likely to have a lower stake factor." **Only 0.64% of accounts were limited, but 57.6% of those were cut to 1-24% of default limits** ([Covers](https://www.covers.com/industry/massachusetts-limiting-sports-betting-prediction-markets-exchanges-sharps-box-september-2025)). The triggers books named were mispriced or stale odds, low-liquidity markets, syndicate betting and data-feed latency ([Birches Health](https://bircheshealth.com/resources/limiting-bettors)). That list describes the project's soft-vs-sharp and props tracks almost exactly. Arizona has 12 live online books, including BetCRIS, which launched there in January 2025 from a Latin American market-making brand. Circa is approved for an early-2027 launch ([LegalSportsReport](https://www.legalsportsreport.com/arizona/); [Covers](https://www.covers.com/industry/betcris-brand-returns-to-usa-with-sportsbook-launch-in-arizona-jan-27-2025)). Taxes are the third constraint. **From tax year 2026 only 90% of gambling losses are deductible**, so $100k won and $100k lost leaves $10k of taxable income ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/nathangoldman/2026/01/13/gambling-tax-alert-new-law-cuts-loss-deductions-bettors-face-big-hit/)). For a strategy earning 2% on turnover, the disallowed 10% of gross losses can exceed real profit. Strategies that churn large gross volume for small margins, such as market making, middling and hedged bonus conversion, take the biggest hit. Whether exchange event-contract gains are even taxed as gambling is unresolved, and that question needs a CPA before anything scales.

The practical consequence is that **edge should be measured per unit of account life, not per bet**. A soft-book account is a depleting asset that pays out once, until the book cuts the stake. Promotions and boosts are the cheapest way to spend it, because they look recreational and are +EV by construction. Practitioners describe "account health matters more than short-term EV" and "betting 'too perfectly' is a red flag" as working rules ([The Modern Edge](https://themodernedge.podbean.com/e/ep3-drew-tabor-ungambled-%E2%80%94-the-stealth-strategy-sharp-bettors-use-to-avoid-limits/)), though no source quantifies how fast any specific book limits. Exchanges are where volume can grow without being cut.

## Props and derivatives reward distribution modeling, but the money is in season-long unders

The documented prop edge is statistical, not informational. Player outcomes are right-skewed and books hang lines at the median, so a bettor comparing a *mean* projection to the line bets "far too many overs" ([Unabated](https://unabated.com/post/the-biggest-mistake-youre-making-when-betting-nfl-player-props)). No book is sharp on props. Pinnacle takes about $250 before gameday. Caesars gives sharp accounts $500 even on openers and sometimes overreacts. FanDuel originates its own numbers, and DraftKings outsources to Swish Analytics ([Establish The Run](https://establishtherun.com/understanding-the-current-ecosystem-of-nfl-player-props/); [Unabated](https://unabated.com/post/the-biggest-mistake-youre-making-when-betting-nfl-player-props)). Weekly props are getting more efficient ([BetSmart](https://betsmart.beehiiv.com/p/efficient-prop-markets)). The project's own results agree: opening prop lines are about as accurate as closing ones, and only receptions shopping survived. The strongest public prop evidence is in **season-long** markets instead. One tracker found **unders won 61% of 604 NFL season-long props in 2021-22, including 74% of QB passing-yard unders**. The mechanism is that injury, benching and trades all add left-tail outcomes ([4for4](https://www.4for4.com/2023/preseason/key-winning-season-long-player-props)). The same author later reported blind unders profitable in five consecutive seasons over 1,392 props ([X](https://x.com/ConnorAllenNFL/status/2081746792849244434)). This comes from one practitioner who has not published ROI, so it is a hypothesis to pre-register. My arithmetic: if 61% held at −115, ROI would be about +14%. Season-long markets also share the regime-lag pattern seen in game totals. NFL unders hit 61.3% through Week 6 of 2023, the highest since 1991, because "the betting market is slow to catch up" with falling scoring ([theScore](https://www.thescore.com/nfl/news/2740613)).

Same-game parlays are the clearest no-go. Books price them with Gaussian copulas or empirical joint frequencies and add **15-25% house edge against 4-5% on singles**. The only analysis found turned up no +EV SGPs ([Wizard of Odds](https://wizardofodds.com/article/same-game-parlays-the-mathematics-of-correlation/)), and measured combo vig was 22.1% at sportsbooks and 26.3% at Kalshi ([RotoWire](https://www.rotowire.com/article/fanduel-kalshi-pull-even-on-nfl-week-3-pricing-134248)). First-half over value is being priced out through juice: lines hold, but favored overs now cost −118 to −122, per a small tout sample ([Jeff's Edge](https://jeffsedge.substack.com/p/sunday-the-books-are-pricing-the)). College teasers do not work either. Across 166 games, no subset beat the 74% per-leg break-even of a −120 two-team teaser ([TheLines](https://www.thelines.com/college-football-teasers-study-2021/)). Pick'em apps imply a flat −122 to −136 per leg, so any leg with a sportsbook no-vig probability above 55-58% is +EV ([Establish The Run](https://establishtherun.com/how-to-beat-pick-em-on-underdog-fantasy/)). For an Arizona resident, though, the state's move against Underdog's license makes this a legal question first and a modeling question second.

The untested derivative territory is mostly college. No source covers CFB prop or derivative efficiency. CFB's flatter margin distribution and extreme tempo differences, from hurry-up offenses to triple-option service academies, are the obvious stress points for first-half and team-total lines derived mechanically from the full-game line. This is a hypothesis with no evidence yet, but the project's `margin_total.py` machinery and its CFB sharp line already make it cheap to price.

## Live betting and news speed are poor fits for an hourly system

The academic case for in-play mispricing is real but comes almost entirely from soccer and basketball exchange data. Augenblick, Lazarus and Thaler, using more than 5 million Betfair transactions, find markets **overreact to early, weak signals and underreact to late, strong ones**. In their experiments early baskets got about 60% too much weight and fourth-quarter baskets about 33% too little ([Berkeley Haas](https://newsroom.haas.berkeley.edu/magazine/spring-2025/the-over-under-conundrum/); [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.09871v4)). EPL exchange prices were inefficient after the first goal: the effect peaked about 20 seconds later and lasted at least five minutes, and gross returns fell from 35-70% to 12-25% over that span ([Angelini et al.](https://www.reading.ac.uk/web/files/economics/emdp201920.pdf)). No study tests NFL or CFB live prices. Against that, US live football is priced from official low-latency data, with Genius Sports the NFL's exclusive distributor ([Covers](https://www.covers.com/industry/nfl-genius-sports-announce-extension-with-multi-year-deal-june-12-2025)). Books use suspensions and bet delays, and one low-reliability industry source puts NFL in-play overround at **6-9% against 2-4% pregame** ([Track360](https://www.track360.io/blog/in-play-betting-margins-operator-economics-2026)). A bettor polling hourly cannot act inside a 20-second window, and latency play draws limits and terms-of-service risk ([BettingUSA](https://www.bettingusa.com/sports/courtsiding/)). Halftime is the one live state static enough to price carefully, and no study of halftime lines exists. The cheapest honest research step is a calibration pilot: compare de-vigged live prices from the project's mid-game snapshots, or free Kalshi trade history, against nflfastR's `vegas_wp`. It should not become a betting track unless it finds a gap well above live margins.

News speed is a similar story. The NFL's report schedule is fixed: practice reports Wednesday to Friday by 4:00 p.m. ET, and game statuses Friday for Sunday games ([NFL](https://operations.nfl.com/media/1818/2015-injury-report-policy.pdf)). College reporting is now just as schedulable. The SEC reports at 8 p.m. ET Wednesday to Friday plus a final report 90 minutes before kickoff, with Probable/Questionable/Doubtful mapped to 75/50/25% ([SI](https://www.si.com/college/florida/explanation-of-the-sec-new-availability-report-rule-01j7gw225xf2)). The Big Ten moved to four weekly reports on September 19, 2026, including "Out-for-first-half" ([theScore](https://fr.thescore.com/ncaaf/news/3587551/sms:)). The Big 12 and ACC report daily, finishing 90 minutes or two hours before kickoff ([CBS Sports](https://www.cbssports.com/college-football/news/big-12-to-mandate-player-availability-reports-for-football-basketball-starting-in-2025-26); [SI](https://www.si.com/college/georgiatech/football/acc-commissioner-jim-phillips-announces-player-availability-reports-will-be-required-in-acc-conference-games-01k0s6d6q12p)). Books say they scan news feeds by algorithm and adjust "in seconds, not minutes" ([Action Network](https://www.actionnetwork.com/education/how-injuries-affect-betting-lines-a-guide-to-market-movement)). The project's finding that CFB QB changes move the sharp line only about 0.24 points after Sunday and Monday says most news is priced before the official reports confirm it. No public study shows an LLM news reader beating a sports betting market ([LSE](https://researchonline.lse.ac.uk/id/eprint/122592) covers equities only). The defensible value of the Haiku pipeline is reading ambiguity, such as "expected to start" language and gameday "game-time decision" resolutions, and using news to **pull resting exchange quotes**. It will not win a race against a bookmaker's keyword scanner. Betting splits also lack support. Fading 70%+ public sides went about 52.5% in NBA data, below break-even ([Paul & Weinbach](https://kylewoodward.com/blog-data/pdfs/references/paul+weinbach-international-journal-of-sport-finance-2008A.pdf)), and fading NFL streak teams went 49.4-51.2% ([Paul, Weinbach & Humphreys](https://sites.ualberta.ca/~econwps/2011/wp2011-16.pdf)). VSiN's splits rules come with no records at all ([VSiN](https://vsin.com/nfl/how-to-be-a-winning-football-bettor-using-draftkings-and-circa-nfl-betting-splits/)).

**Rank 1 is a repair, not a new signal, and it comes first because every other price track inherits its error.** The project's fair prices use basic multiplicative normalization, in `exchanges.py` and `features.vegas_prob`. The devig literature calls that "the least accurate" method and recommends power, odds-ratio, margin-weights-proportional-to-odds or Shin, which model the favorite-longshot bias ([implied vignette](https://ftp.fau.de/cran/web/packages/implied/vignettes/introduction.html)). Multiplicative devig is adequate only for odds in the 35-65% range ([Data Golf](https://datagolf.com/how-sharp-are-bookmakers)). Moneyline v2 accepts prices from −1000 to +1000, so its underdog bets are exactly where an overstated longshot fair probability makes a soft price look falsely +EV. Entry EV and closing CLV share the same bias, so part of it cancels in CLV. Realized ROI and Kelly stakes do not get that offset. The reference set needs the same scrutiny: Data Golf finds BetOnline copies Pinnacle, so a median of Pinnacle, LowVig and BetOnline is mostly one opinion, and regressing the closing no-vig on each book's earlier no-vig in the project's own history would set independence weights. For CFB, Circa opens the market and BetCRIS is legal in Arizona. Whether any affordable feed carries BetCRIS-AZ prices is unverified. The Unabated API's sharp consensus starts at $3,000 a month ([sportsapis.dev](https://sportsapis.dev/apis/unabated)), and OddsPapi claims free access to Asian sharp books ([OddsPapi](https://oddspapi.io/blog/?p=2926)), a vendor claim to verify before relying on it. The test costs nothing. Score each devig method's log loss on 2020-25 Pinnacle closes, then re-grade the frozen ML ledgers under the winner without changing the rules.

**Rank 2 is the only idea on the list that scales past soft-book limits.** The project already prices every game against Pinnacle and runs an hourly watch. The step it has not taken is resting limit orders on Kalshi, or ProphetX for college, slightly better than fair for the counterparty, instead of crossing the spread. My arithmetic: a fill 1¢ inside a 50¢ fair price earns about 2% of stake gross, less roughly 0.9% maker fee on Kalshi. The unknown is adverse selection. Resting orders get picked off when news hits, which is where `news_llm` and free cancellations become an execution tool instead of a logging tool. The longshot finding suggests resting quotes on the favorite side of lopsided contracts, but the evidence is mostly non-sports and needs a sports-specific check first. Paper trading cannot simulate queue position honestly, so the realistic test is a small, pre-registered live allocation sized for the legal risk, together with a full log of quotes, fills and the Pinnacle price at fill time.

**Ranks 3-5 are cheap and well matched to the project's discipline.** Boosts are +EV against a fair price by definition. A 10% boost on a −110 coin flip is worth +$4.55 per $100 ([JuiceReel](https://juicereel.beehiiv.com/p/ranking-live-sportsbook-promotions)), and max stakes of $10-50 make this a few hundred dollars a season rather than thousands. Its larger value is camouflage: it makes CLV-positive accounts look recreational. Bonus bets convert at about 50-70% of face with expert play ([JuiceReel](https://juicereel.beehiiv.com/p/ranking-live-sportsbook-promotions)), and hedging at a low-fee exchange should beat hedging at another −110 book. Under the 2026 tax rule, conversion turnover now carries a hidden cost that the engine's EV should include. Season-long unders need a pre-registered rule frozen before next August's markets open. For FBS wind, a Football Study Hall study of about 7,300 games found unders hit 54.7% above 10 mph and about 60% above 17 mph, roughly 1.5 points of unpriced total per 10 mph. It used *observed* wind and asked "How much do you trust weather predictions?" ([Football Study Hall](https://www.footballstudyhall.com/2018/6/25/17500384/football-betting-windy-conditions-effect)), so the forecast-based rule the project already runs for the NFL is the right structure to copy.

**Ranks 6-12 are worth a frozen rule or a measurement, not engineering effort.** CFB derivatives get no direct evidence and inherit low limits. Survivor contests are attractive because they do not limit and reuse calibrated weekly probabilities. Holiday slates of 3-4 games, where a 7-point favorite can draw more than 50% of picks, are where leverage concentrates ([PoolGenius](https://poolgenius.teamrankings.com/circa-survivor-picks/articles/navigate-sharp-nfl-survivor-pools-circa-survivor/)). Entry logistics and prize economics for an Arizona resident are unverified. The CFB ensemble and dynamic shrinkage come from the earlier modeling review. They improve the opener-time prior, not the close-beating claim, and Connelly's position weights for returning production would sharpen the CFB preseason prior: OL 39.6%, receiving 35.0%, QB 22.3%, with transfers credited at their previous team's production and lower-division transfers at half ([ESPN via ABC7](https://abc7chicago.com/post/what-returning-production-looks-like-2026-college-football-season/18754857/)). The NFL preseason is openly sharp-dominated, and a Westgate oddsmaker says lines "can move seven points" on QB-snap and coach-motivation news, but limits are $2,000-3,000 ([Review-Journal](https://develop.reviewjournal.com/?p=2376396)). Service-academy unders went 44-9-1, but the market has already cut those totals from the 50s to around 30, and the trend is a famous one with obvious selection bias ([Covers](https://www.covers.com/ncaaf/service-academy-betting-trends-over-under-total)). One more standing monitor belongs in this tier. Totals lag rule changes: CFB overs fell to 47.2% after the 2006 clock change and recovered by November ([Paulson](https://www.westga.edu/~bquest/2008/football08.pdf)). That makes the first weeks after any future clock, overtime or kickoff change a recurring, pre-registerable window rather than a permanent edge.

Several ideas should stay off the list given the evidence. SGPs carry 15-25% hold. Splits-fading has no out-of-sample record. CFB and totals teasers fail. Sub-minute steam-chasing needs a feed and execution speed the system lacks, and it burns accounts. Award and Heisman futures have no quantified evidence and high overround. Pick'em apps are a legal question in Arizona. Cross-venue arbitrage between prediction markets "typically exist[s] only for a few seconds" ([QuantPedia](https://quantpedia.com/systematic-edges-in-prediction-markets/)), and the project found sportsbook arbitrage worth about 0.1 unit a season.

## Conclusion

The evidence shifts the project's main question from "where is a better signal" to "where can the signal it already has be cashed for longest." The Pinnacle-anchored fair price is the project's core asset. It can be used three ways: to pick off soft books, to quote on exchanges, and to choose which promotions to take. Only the first is built, and it is the use books profile and limit fastest. The exchange quote and the promo engine stretch the same edge across venues that tolerate winners or that make winning look recreational. That is how a Walters-style operation, built on many accounts and careful order flow, translates into code for a single automated bettor.

Two things will decide whether this works, and neither is modeling. The first is legal and tax plumbing. An adverse Arizona ruling would close the exchange route outright, and the 90% loss-deduction rule turns low-margin, high-turnover strategies into possible tax losers, so EV should be computed after tax and per dollar of account life, not per bet. The second is measurement hygiene in the fair price itself. Until the devig and reference weighting are checked, the project cannot tell how much of its moneyline CLV comes from soft-book lag and how much from a normalization artifact on underdogs. That check costs an afternoon and should run before any new track is frozen.
