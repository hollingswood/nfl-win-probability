## Conclusions

- A verdict: NO opener edge. Both frozen opener rules FAIL the pre-registered bar on 2023-25 (one run): ml_any_sharp2 (market-only soft-vs-sharp >=2% at the opener) n=65, CLV[all] +0.90% p=0.28; ml_model5_clean n=68, CLV[all] +1.62% p=0.057 (nflverse +2.08% p=0.02; needs p<0.025 on the price close).
- Openers show MORE soft-vs-sharp gaps (2023-25: 14% of opener snapshots have a my_books ML price >=2% over the BetOnline/lowvig no-vig vs 7-10% later; dev 9.5% vs 6-9%), but those gaps do not predict the close: on dev the market-only opener bets had ~0 CLV (+0.04%) while the same rules at later snapshots had +2-5%. At the opener the 'sharp' book (lowvig/betonlineag; Circa/Bookmaker absent in region-us data) is no sharper than consensus (opener log loss 0.6168 sharp vs 0.6169 consensus; close 0.612).
- Lines move modestly opener->close: mean |win-prob move| 3.3-3.8 pts, spread moved >=1 pt in ~50% of games, >=2 pts in ~20-25%, crossed 3/7 or sign in ~14-17%.
- Model vs openers: the model's gap does predict the move to the close more at the opener (slope 0.21-0.25) than at 1-3 days (0.06) or gameday (~0.02), but much of that is look-ahead: replay_predictions uses final-report QB/injury inputs and results of games not yet played at the opener (late Sunday/SNF/MNF). On clean rows (no QB news, prior games final) the slope falls to 0.16-0.18 and the tradable rules are weak (ml_model5_clean fails; sp_model3_clean CLV ~0 or negative). Treat any model-at-opener number as an upper bound.
- Spread soft-vs-sharp rules keep looking good vs the nflverse spread_line (+1-2%) and bad vs the price-based close (-1 to -2%), at openers and later: the known spread_line/juice artefact, not an edge.
- B (2025 wk 3-12 Sunday games, descriptive; region-us books only): adding hourly checks Sat 15:00-Sun 16:00 on top of the existing schedule adds 0.3 C2 bets/week (3 games), 0.6 C4 bets/week (6 games) and 0.3 C1/week; their CLV[all] is positive but small-sample (+1.3% / +1.5%, n=3/6). Inside the window hourly sees 21 C2 and 28 C4 (game,side) opportunities vs 5-7 bets at our existing in-window runs; 13/21 and 18/28 were never visible at an existing run, but most of those games were already bet earlier in the week by the existing schedule. Every hourly opportunity start had CLV[all] +2.9% (C2) / +3.9% (C4).
- Opportunities are sticky, not fleeting: median run 5 h (C2) / 6.5 h (C4) across consecutive hourly snapshots; ~24-25% last only one hourly snapshot; ~50% last >= 6 h (runs are censored at the 26-h window edges). The ML gap share is ~15% of games Saturday and ~10-12% Sunday morning.
- Credits: existing schedule ~14 runs/week x 4 credits ~ 240 credits/month. Hourly 24/7 in season ~ 2,900 credits/month; every 15 min 24/7 ~ 11,600. With 20k credits/month, hourly checks are affordable; sub-hourly checks are not justified by the persistence data (most opportunities last several hours).
