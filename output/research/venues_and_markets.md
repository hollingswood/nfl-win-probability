# Venues, Markets, Data & Limits: Briefing for an Arizona-based Automated NFL Bettor

As of 2026-09-30. Prepared from web sources; every factual claim cites a URL. Items I could not verify are marked **UNVERIFIED**. Where a source is a vendor or affiliate site, it's noted. Numbers marked "(own arithmetic)" are my calculations from the cited inputs, not sourced facts.

**Bottom line up front**

- The "cost of betting" for an Arizona resident fell in 2026, but **through CFTC-regulated exchanges whose legal status in Arizona could still change**, not through a cheaper licensed sportsbook. **ProphetX** (2% commission on net winnings) and **Kalshi as a maker** (a fee of about 25% of the taker fee, and no spread paid) are the cheapest legal-for-now places to execute. **Novig is excluded in AZ, Crypto.com pulled out of AZ, Sporttrade left AZ, and Circa isn't licensed in AZ.**
- **Taking liquidity on Kalshi is not cheap.** A weekly price study measured Kalshi's straight-bet "vig" at about 4.3%, roughly the same as FanDuel's. The savings only show up if you **post limit (maker) orders**.
- Arizona's fight with Kalshi is on a knife-edge. A federal preliminary injunction (May 5, 2026) currently blocks Arizona from enforcing its laws. But the Ninth Circuit ruled for the states in the Nevada case (Aug 28, 2026), and Arizona is now asking for that injunction to be vacated.
- **Federal tax change (2026):** only 90% of gambling losses are deductible. For a high-turnover, small-edge strategy this can create taxable "phantom income" bigger than your real profit.

### 1.3 What this means for the soft-vs-sharp moneyline strategy

- **CLV accounting check (method note, not a sourced fact):** if your +1–2.5% CLV is already measured as *your bet price vs the de-vigged sharp close*, the book's vig is already in that number, so it is your EV. If it's measured against the *vigged* close, subtract roughly half the hold. Confirm which one before comparing venues.
- **On exchanges you can't "shop the soft book."** Your edge has to come from either (a) **taking stale exchange orders** that lag a Pinnacle move, or (b) **posting maker orders** at or just inside the sharp fair price and letting flow fill you. Taking liquidity on Kalshi costs about what FanDuel's vig does ([RotoWire](https://www.rotowire.com/article/fanduel-kalshi-pull-even-on-nfl-week-3-pricing-134248)). Making liquidity costs ~0.9% of stake at 50¢ ([pm.wiki](https://pm.wiki/learn/kalshi-fees-explained)). ProphetX costs ~1% at even money ([PredictionScout](https://predictionscout.com/reviews/prophetx-review/)). **UNVERIFIED:** whether Kalshi or ProphetX prices lag Pinnacle by enough, and for long enough, to be taken profitably. Test this with the free Kalshi feed before committing money.
- Kalshi parlays/combos are expensive: 26.3% vig vs 22.1% at sportsbooks ([RotoWire](https://www.rotowire.com/article/fanduel-kalshi-pull-even-on-nfl-week-3-pricing-134248)).

## 3. Promotions and boosts at AZ-licensed books

**Market context.** Promotions are shrinking and getting more complicated. In 2026 they shifted toward multi-day, multi-bet structures, e.g. FanDuel's five-day "bet reset" and Caesars' ten-bet profit-doubling ([rg.org](https://rg.org/news/gambling-industry/why-sportsbook-welcome-bonuses-look-different-in-2026)). ACSI 2026 found sportsbook satisfaction fell 3% "as bonuses dry up" ([Gambling.com, Aug 28 2026](https://www.gambling.com/us/news/us-sportsbook-satisfaction-falls-3-as-bonuses-dry-up)). Arizona taxes online sportsbooks at 10% and lets operators deduct promotions only up to a cap (10% of revenue, with a smaller allowance from 2025) ([AGA State of the States](https://www.americangaming.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Arizona_Overview.pdf)). That gives AZ books some room to keep running promos. **Note:** "ESPN Bet" in AZ is now **theScore Bet** ([BetArizona](https://www.betarizona.com/); [ESPN BET support](https://espnbet.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/40921200115725-ESPN-BET-Rebrand-to-theScore-Bet)).

**Mechanics and typical value:**

- **Bonus bets** (stake not returned). Typical conversion is about **70%** of face value, best at +300 to +500 hedged at another book ([DarkHorse Odds](https://about.darkhorseodds.com/guides/how-to-convert-a-bonus-bet)). Up to ~90% "is usually as good as you'll get," using very long odds hedged at about the opposite price ([Action Network](https://www.actionnetwork.com/legal-online-sports-betting/how-to-use-free-money-bets-promos-bonuses)). OddsJam claims 95–98% "efficiency" for its Promo Finder ([RotoWire review](https://www.rotowire.com/betting/oddsjam-review)). That is a vendor claim, likely measured on a different basis, so **UNVERIFIED** as a conversion rate. **A low-fee exchange hedge** (ProphetX at 2% of net winnings) should convert better than hedging at a -110 book. That follows from the fees above (own inference), not from a measured source.
- **No-sweat / second-chance bets.** A losing stake comes back as bonus bets, not cash ([BookmakersReview, Jan 2026](https://www.bookmakersreview.com/betting/bonus/no-sweat-bets/)). One worked example for a $1,000 no-sweat bet gives EV of about −0.8% at -500, **+18% at -110**, and **+49% at +400** ([On the Juice](https://juicereel.beehiiv.com/p/how-to-maximize-the-no-sweat-bet)). The method is to use a long-odds first bet (+300 or longer).
- **Profit/odds boosts.** Usually +10–25%, and max stakes are usually **$10–$50** ([Betstamp](https://www.betstamp.com/education/evaluating-odds-boost-promos-are-they-worth-it)). Own arithmetic: a 25% profit boost on a fair ~50% side priced at -110 pays 0.909 × 1.25 = 1.136 profit per unit, so EV ≈ 0.5 × 1.136 − 0.5 ≈ **+6.8% of stake**. On a fair +100 line it would be +12.5%. At $25–$50 caps, that is dollars per boost, not hundreds.
- **Fanatics FanCash.** Earned at 0.01%–10% of stake depending on odds, and converts 1:1 into bonus bets that expire in 90 days ([SBD, Nov 2025](https://www.sportsbettingdime.com/sportsbooks/fanatics/fancash/)).

**Boost/promo trackers:** [Odds Boosts Tracker](https://www.sportsbettingoddscalculator.com/odds-boosts), [OddsCrowd boost aggregator](https://oddscrowd.com/odds-boosts), [CrazyNinjaOdds boost calculator](https://crazyninjaodds.com/site/tools/odds-boost.aspx), and OddsJam (Gold about $6.60/day monthly or $5.40/day annual, includes "promotional conversion") ([RotoWire](https://www.rotowire.com/betting/oddsjam-review)). **No machine-readable boost API found (UNVERIFIED).** The Odds API does not advertise boosts.

## 4. Props and same-game parlays

**Efficiency evidence (mostly practitioner, little peer-reviewed):**

- "There is no *sharp* book for props." Caesars and FanDuel are described as the most efficient domestic prop books, and the prop offering is "too large every week for one book to efficiently price them all" ([Establish The Run, 2023](https://establishtherun.com/understanding-the-current-ecosystem-of-nfl-player-props/)).
- Pinnacle keeps low prop limits ($250–$500) compared with game lines. "Large edges exist in NFL player props." "$500 bet sizes fly under the radar at many of the bigger U.S. books," but books still limit identified edge-holders ([Unabated, Jul 31 2026](https://unabated.com/articles/the-biggest-mistake-youre-making-when-betting-nfl-player-props)). A common modeling error is using a **mean** projection against a line set at the **median** (same source).
- SGPs: the claimed typical house edge is **15–25%** vs 4–5% on singles, priced with copula or empirical-frequency methods. The source cites no data, so treat it as **UNVERIFIED** ([Wizard of Odds, Aug 2026](https://wizardofodds.com/article/same-game-parlays-the-mathematics-of-correlation/)). Measured parlay/combo vig: sportsbooks 22.1%, Kalshi 26.3% ([RotoWire](https://www.rotowire.com/article/fanduel-kalshi-pull-even-on-nfl-week-3-pricing-134248)). Illinois' 16.33% overall hold (May 2026) is attributed to parlays doing "the heavy lifting on margin" ([rg.org](https://rg.org/news/gambling-industry/illinois-may-2026-sports-betting-revenue-report)).
- Academic: in college football, favorites covering correlates with the over. Books were "too conservative" in refusing those correlated parlays ([Davis, Dawson & Krieger, J. Prediction Markets 2018](https://www.ubplj.org/index.php/jpm/article/view/1562)). This is old evidence and not NFL-specific.
- **Which prop markets are softest:** no rigorous source found (**UNVERIFIED**). The thin-liquidity note on ProphetX props ([PredictionScout](https://predictionscout.com/reviews/prophetx-review/)) and Pinnacle's low prop limits ([Unabated](https://unabated.com/articles/the-biggest-mistake-youre-making-when-betting-nfl-player-props)) point to the long tail of props being least efficient. **How quickly prop bettors get limited: no quantitative public data found (UNVERIFIED).**

## 6. Limits at sportsbooks

- **Massachusetts data, the only regulator dataset found:** **0.64%** of bettors across seven books were limited. 57.6% of limited accounts were cut to 1–24% of the default max, and 12.7% to under 1%. Consistent winners were more likely to be limited ([SBC Americas, Sep 30 2025](https://sbcamericas.com/2025/09/30/mgc-data-limiting-bettors-ma/)). Since **June 1, 2026**, MA requires a specific reason within 48 hours, the first rule of its kind in the US ([SCCG](https://sccgmanagement.com/sccg-articles/2026/06/02/massachusetts-becomes-first-state-mandate-specific/)). **Arizona has no comparable rule that I could find (UNVERIFIED).**
- **Speed of limiting:** no rigorous public data found. The evidence is anecdotal, e.g. a DraftKings account cut to $50 max after one +180 futures win ([forum anecdote](https://athleticbarnchicago.com/threads/draftkings-account-limited-bears-wins-bet.2143/)). Treat "how fast" as **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Circa** doesn't limit winners and starts accounts at $5k max, but it isn't in AZ ([BettingUSA](https://www.bettingusa.com/sports/reviews/circa-sports/)). The exchanges (Kalshi, ProphetX) work on position limits or accountability levels rather than limiting winners ([OddsShopper](https://www.oddsshopper.com/articles/prediction-markets/kalshi-position-limits); [PredictionScout](https://predictionscout.com/reviews/prophetx-review/)).
- **Implications:** 12 AZ books ([BetArizona](https://www.betarizona.com/)) give you a finite set of soft accounts. Spend them where the edge per dollar of "account life" is highest (promos and boosts, soft-book ML steals) and move the volume to exchanges.

## 7. Tax: a structural cost that matters more than vig at small edges

- Starting tax year 2026, **only 90% of gambling losses are deductible**. Example: $100k won and $100k lost leaves $10k of "phantom" taxable income ([NATP](https://www.natptax.com/news-insights/blog/will-the-obbba-gambling-deduction-change-be-reversed/); [Forbes, Jan 2026](https://www.forbes.com/sites/nathangoldman/2026/01/13/gambling-tax-alert-new-law-cuts-loss-deductions-bettors-face-big-hit/)). The FAIR BET Act (H.R. 4304, Jul 2025) would restore full deductibility; it had **not passed** as of the sources found.
- For a ~2%-edge strategy with large turnover, gross losses dwarf net profit, so the 10% disallowance can exceed real profit (own inference).
- **UNVERIFIED and important:** whether CFTC event-contract gains (Kalshi, ProphetX) are taxed as gambling, as Section 1256 contracts, or as ordinary income. Get a CPA opinion before scaling.
