How to read this

Factor bars show how much each input moved the prediction away from a coin flip, in approximate percentage points, and which team it favors. Home field includes the league-wide home edge, which is near zero at neutral sites.

Vegas is a very strong benchmark. Matching it over a full season would be a good result; a large disagreement usually means the market knows something the model doesn't, like an injury announced after the data refresh.
