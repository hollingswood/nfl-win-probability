Sportsbook promos are the most reliable edge most bettors get. This values a promo against the fair (no-vig) price from the sharpest books we track, not against the promo book's own odds. Expected cash per bonus bet. Longer prices keep more of it, until the vig on longshots takes it back.

No-sweat bet

Refunded as a bonus bet if it loses. EV counts the refund at your conversion rate.

Profit boost

The boost multiplies the profit only; a max-winnings cap limits it.

How this is computed
  • d = decimal odds (+150 → 2.50, −120 → 1.833), S = stake, p = fair win probability, win = profit if it wins = min((d−1)·S, max winnings).
  • Fair p, moneyline: median no-vig of Pinnacle, LowVig and BetOnline; if missing, Pinnacle alone, then LowVig/BetOnline, then the all-book consensus. The result always names the source. Spreads and totals: your books' own no-vig at the exact number you pick (median across the books posting it). That is less sharp than the moneyline source. Whole-number lines can push; a push usually refunds the stake or token, which pulls EV toward zero.
  • Profit boost b: d′ = 1 + (d−1)(1+b), capped by max winnings; EV = p·win − (1−p)·S; break-even p = S / (S + win).
  • Bonus bet (stake not returned): EV = p·(d−1)·S, shown as a share of the bonus, p·(d−1).
  • No-sweat (refund as a bonus bet if it loses, conversion c): EV = p·(d−1)·S − (1−p)·S + (1−p)·c·S; break-even p = (1−c)·S / (win + (1−c)·S).
  • Deposit / bet match (bonus B = match % × S up to the max, paid as bonus bets worth c each, rollover R × B wagered at this price): EV = p·win − (1−p)·S + c·B − R·B·(1 − p·d).
  • Hedge to lock a bonus bet: bet H = win / d₂ in cash on the other side at its best price d₂. Either result then pays win·(d₂−1)/d₂: that amount is locked, with no risk.
  • Verdict: Take it at EV ≥ +3% of stake (bonus bets: ≥ 70% of face value); Marginal from 0 to 3% (60–70%); Pass below that.

    How to read this

    Edge vs. factors. The factor bars explain how the model arrived at its own line, in points. They are not edges: Vegas sees most of the same things. The edge comes only from the gap between the model's line and Vegas's line, and the bet math deliberately shrinks that gap toward Vegas before calculating our chance. A game can have big factors and zero edge.

    Moneyline grades are the predicted closing-line value of the best price for each side, from a model frozen on 2020–22 lines (A+ ≥ 2.5%, A ≥ 1.5%, B ≥ 0.5%, else C). On unseen 2023–25 lines the realized CLV rose with the letter, but only A+ showed an edge (+2.3% CLV); A and B were about zero. The grade is a label: it never decides or sizes a track's bet. Games with no grade have no price that is positive against any fair reference. Spread grades are the original points scheme: they start from the edge and subtract for warning signs (the model far from the market, a QB change, the wrong side of 3 or 7, the line moving against us). In 2020–25 tests they ranked spread bets in the right order; spread A+ showed +2.2% CLV, a borderline result. Totals grades are the predicted closing-line value of each over/under offer at your books, from a model frozen on 2020–22 lines (A+ ≥ 0.0%, A ≥ −1%, B ≥ −2%, else C; a −110 bet at the consensus number is about −4.5%). On 2023–25 lines A+ gained +1.5% CLV and A, B and C all lost value; A+ is mostly early-week unders. Totals are graded within 7 days of kickoff. Also a label only.

    Factor bars show how many points of predicted margin each input is worth and which team it favors. Home field is about 2 points, and near zero at neutral sites. Injuries count every non-QB player listed Out or Doubtful, weighted by their recent share of snaps; reports come out Wednesday to Friday, so early-week numbers assume everyone is healthy.

    Model total comes from a simple display-only model (each team's recent points scored and allowed, offensive and defensive efficiency, league scoring level, dome). On 2020–25 it missed the actual total by 10.6 points on average, against 10.3 for the closing Vegas total, so it is context, not an edge, and no track bets on it. AI-read news is pulled from public feeds by an AI reader; it is not used by the model or any track. ✓ verified means the player is on that team's roster (Sleeper); "team unclear" means the AI's team did not match. After games, each signal is checked against who actually played and started; AI QB news may feed the model's QB availability only after 25 such QB signals are at least 95% right (rule frozen 2026-10-02).

    Travel, time zones, body clock, weather and Next Gen Stats were each tested as model inputs and did not improve out-of-sample accuracy, so they appear on the cards as context and are not part of the prediction. Live odds come from multiple sportsbooks when an Odds API key is set; "edge" is the expected return at the best available price using the model blended with the market (the same probability the paper bets use), and the backtest above shows those edges have not been profitable against closing lines.

    Vegas is a very strong benchmark. Matching it over a full season would be a good result; a large disagreement usually means the market knows something the model doesn't, like an injury announced after the data refresh.
