What the testing says

The model sees every public stat we could get: opponent-adjusted points, expected points added per play, success rate, explosiveness, rushing and passing efficiency, offensive-line yards, standard- and passing-down efficiency, roster talent and returning production. It still trails the closing line by about half a point to a point and a half per game. That's the same lesson as the NFL: the edges are in prices, not predictions.
