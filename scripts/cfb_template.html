Best places for a bonus bet this week

A bonus bet returns only the profit, so it keeps the most value at longer prices: expected cash per $1 of bonus = fair win chance × (decimal odds − 1). Prices +150 to +500 at your books, valued at Pinnacle's fair price. Treat the longest prices with care: in 2021–25, +300 and longer college dogs won a little less often than Pinnacle's price implied.

What the testing says

How to read this

Grades compare the best price at your books with Pinnacle's line at the same moment, at any number (college key numbers 3, 7, 10, 14, 17, 21, 24, 28 are built in). A+ = the price beats Pinnacle by 4%+, A = 2–4%, B = 0–2%, C = worse than Pinnacle. On 2021–25 lines the grades lined up with what happened at the close: A+ offers beat Pinnacle's closing line by about 2–7.5% depending on the market, A by 0.8–2.6%, B about zero to +1%, and C lost 1.3–3.7%. Closing line value is the test because results alone are too noisy: after 300 bets profit is still uncertain by about ±6%, closing line value by about ±0.5%. Probabilities remove the vig with Shin's method, which treats long shots more cautiously than simple scaling. No grade means Pinnacle hasn't posted the game yet (it posts most college games Sunday or Monday) or doesn't list it.

Tracks. The shop-vs-sharp track bets spreads graded A+ and totals or moneylines graded A or better, priced −200 to +200, at any Arizona book, from 7 days to 60 minutes before kickoff, one bet per game per market; bets at books where you have no account are marked. The college moneyline track uses only your three books and prices −300 to +300. Grades are only given between −200 and +200: long shots were never validated, and in 2021–25 the ones that beat Pinnacle still lost money. The body-clock track tests rule F2 at your request even though it failed its screen.

Model numbers come from a ratings model built on opponent-adjusted efficiency, success rate, explosiveness, line play, talent and returning production. It picks winners nearly as often as Vegas but misses the final margin by more, so it is context only. Odds refresh hourly Thursday–Saturday and every 3 hours otherwise.
